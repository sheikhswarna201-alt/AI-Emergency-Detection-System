AI Emergency Detection & Alert System

Intelligent Monitoring System for Individuals Living Alone

Overview

The AI Emergency Detection & Alert System is an intelligent software prototype to increase the safety of older individuals and those living alone.

It provides a demonstration of how automated monitoring could be used to detect any potential emergencies, track the users’ location and coordinate responses from those who can help.

Problem

Individuals living alone (especially older individuals and those with medical conditions) may encounter emergencies when there is no one around to help them. They may also struggle to get to their phone or ask for assistance.

Solution

A smart monitoring system which can detect any emergencies automatically and provide a demonstration of how alerts can be coordinated to ensure individuals can get help from those close by they trust.

Features

Health Monitoring – Simulated heart rate and room temperature monitoring.

Activity Monitoring – Simulated mobility and Activity Monitoring.

Emergency Detection – Demonstration of how falls, abnormal health readings and long periods of inactivity can be detected.

Location Tracking – Map showing where emergencies have occurred.

Responders – Responders will be organised based on proximity, availability and estimated time of arrival.

Alerts – Demonstration of how alerts can be escalated to further contacts.

Activity Log – A log of monitoring and emergency activity.

Demo mode – Ability to view different emergency scenarios.

Limitations

This is just a proof of concept software prototype.

There is no connection to actual medical sensors or emergency services. All monitoring and emergencies are simulated.
